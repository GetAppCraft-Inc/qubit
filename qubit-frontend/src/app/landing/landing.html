How it works

From sign-up to submit in four steps.

  1. 01

    Register

    Email and a password. Confirm your address from the link we send, and you're in.

  2. 02

    Pick a slot

    Every mock runs in scheduled slots. Choose one that fits your week — and reschedule any time before it starts.

  3. 03

    Print your hall ticket

    A unique number and barcode, ready the moment you register. Invigilators can look it up in a second.

  4. 04

    Sit the paper

    Full screen, one attempt, server-timed. Submit when you're done, or the clock submits for you.

Integrity

If something's off, the paper closes.

Qubit watches the exam window. Leave it — switch tabs, minimise, drop out of full screen, open developer tools — and the attempt is flagged on the spot. Go past the exam's tolerance and it ends, with the reason on record for the invigilator.

  • Tab switches & minimising
  • Window focus loss
  • Leaving full screen
  • A second display
  • Developer tools
  • Copy, paste & right-click
  • Reopening in another tab or device
  • Going offline for more than 45 s

A browser can't see the rest of the room. For high-stakes papers, pair Qubit with a supervised centre — the hall-ticket barcode is there for exactly that.

For coaching institutes

Set the paper. Run the slots. Watch it live.

Question bank

Single-correct, multi-correct and numeric-answer questions with per-question marking. Paste a batch as JSON when you're moving an existing paper over.

Slots & seats

Schedule as many sittings as you need, cap each one, and set a late-entry window. Candidates choose; you see who's where.

Live monitor

Every running attempt on one screen — who's online, how much time is left, every flag as it happens. End an attempt with a reason in two clicks.

Results that teach

Students get rank, percentile, subject-wise accuracy and a question-by-question review once every slot has closed. Staff get score distributions and the questions that tripped most people up.

Open quizzes & webhooks

Not every test needs a lockdown. Run homework and practice as open quizzes, and push every finished attempt to your own systems with signed webhooks.
